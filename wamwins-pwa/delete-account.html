Delete your WAMWins account and data

WAMWins by DigitalDevWizard

You can delete your WAMWins account and all of its data at any time. Deleting is free on every plan.

Delete inside the app

  1. Open WAMWins and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon on the Home screen).
  3. Tap Delete all data on this device and confirm.

To delete only health details (allergies, intolerances, diets, health goals, food rules and preferences), go to Settings › Privacy & sharing › Delete health details.

Delete without the app

Email admin@wamwins.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my WAMWins account”. You can also use Settings › Privacy & sharing › Make a privacy request › Delete my information. We confirm your identity, then delete your data and reply within 45 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email and sign-in details).
  • Your household, family member profiles and their health details.
  • Kitchens, storage, inventory, meal history, reference photos, and any shared-access settings.
  • Copies in cloud backups, if you used cloud sync or backup, are removed when those backups expire.

What we keep

We keep a record of the consents you gave or withdrew (dates and document versions, not your health details), because the law may require us to show them. These records are kept for up to 6 years after the account is deleted, then erased.

Deleting cannot be undone. If you want a copy of your data first, use Settings › Privacy & sharing › Download a copy of my data.
