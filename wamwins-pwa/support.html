Common questions

How do I add food?

Open Inventory and tap Add food. WAMWins Basic uses manual entry.

Who can see my family’s health details?

Only you, the people who manage a child’s profile, and anyone you give access to in Settings › Privacy & sharing, for the time you choose.

How do I download or delete my data?

See Delete your account, or go to Settings › Privacy & sharing.

Is WAMWins medical advice?

No. WAMWins is a general wellness tool and is not a medical device. Always check product labels. In an allergic emergency, call 911.
