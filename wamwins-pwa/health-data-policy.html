Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

GOITSOKAY, LLC (DigitalDevWizard) · WAMWins · Effective November 19, 2026 · Version 2026-11-19

Effective November 19, 2026. This policy applies to consumer health data under the Washington My Health My Data Act, Nevada law and similar state laws.

Consumer health data we collect

  • Allergies and intolerances
  • Dietary requirements (for example vegetarian, gluten-free, halal)
  • Health-related food goals (for example lower sodium, higher protein)
  • Foods to avoid or prioritize, per meal
  • Meal preferences and the meals you record

Why we collect and use it

Only to match meals and food to the needs you enter, and to show those needs to the people you choose. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not infer medical conditions from it.

Where it comes from

From you, and from people you allow to edit a profile. A parent or guardian enters a child’s information.

Who receives it

  • People you give access to, for the categories and time you choose.
  • Service providers (processors) that host and secure our systems, only to provide the service to us. Categories: cloud hosting, data backup, security monitoring.
  • No affiliates and no data brokers receive it.

Your choices

Requests: Privacy & sharing in the app, or admin@wamwins.com. We respond within 45 days. You may appeal a refusal by replying to our answer; if you are not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general.

  • We ask for your consent before we collect health data, and separately before it is shared with anyone you choose.
  • Withdraw consent, see or delete the data, and ask for the list of people and companies that received it, in Privacy & sharing or by email.
  • We never sell consumer health data. Selling it would require your separate signed authorization, which we do not request.
  • We do not use location to geofence health care facilities.
