Privacy Notice

GOITSOKAY, LLC (DigitalDevWizard) · WAMWins · Effective November 19, 2026 · Version 2026-11-19

Effective November 19, 2026. This notice explains how GOITSOKAY, LLC, doing business as DigitalDevWizard (“DigitalDevWizard”, “we”), collects, uses, shares and protects personal information in WAMWins, and the choices and rights you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a protected one-way code of your password (never the password itself).
  • Household details: the household name, the people you add (names, relationship, age group) and who does the cooking.
  • Consumer health data, only with your consent: allergies, intolerances, dietary requirements, health-related food goals, foods to avoid or prioritize, meal preferences and meal history. See the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.
  • Kitchen details: kitchens, storage, appliances, kitchenware and the food you enter.
  • Photos you choose to add as reference pictures. They are stored, never analyzed, in WAMWins Basic.
  • Voice commands, only in plans that include them and only after you turn them on. See “Voice commands” below.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the app safely, such as the app version and error reports.

How we use it

We do not sell personal information. We do not use your health data for advertising, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use your health data to train artificial intelligence models.

  • To run the features you use: setting up your household, inventory, and matching meals to your family’s saved food needs.
  • To keep accounts and data secure, prevent misuse and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to your requests.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored on your device. When you use online features such as cloud backup or signing in on other devices, it is also stored in encrypted form on servers in North America.

Who can see it

  • You, and the people you choose. Health details are visible only to the person they describe, the parent or guardian who manages a child’s profile, and anyone you give access to for the time you choose. You can end access at any time.
  • Service providers that host and secure our systems or send account emails, under contracts that allow them to use the information only to provide those services to us.
  • Others when the law requires it, for example a valid court order, or to protect someone’s safety. If we can, we will tell you first.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or merged, under the same promises; we will tell you before your information becomes subject to a different policy.

Your rights and choices

To make a request, use Privacy & sharing in the app or email admin@wamwins.com. We respond within 45 days and may verify your identity first. You may use an authorized agent. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

  • See and download a copy of your information (Privacy & sharing → Download a copy).
  • Correct information in the app, or ask us to correct it.
  • Delete health details, a family member’s profile, or your whole account and household.
  • Withdraw a consent at any time. Withdrawing does not affect use before it, and other features keep working.
  • Ask which people and companies received your consumer health data.
  • Appeal if we decline a request. You can also contact your state attorney general.

How long we keep it

  • Health details: while your consent is active. Deleted within 30 days after you withdraw consent or ask us to delete them.
  • Account and household information: while your account is open. Deleted within 30 days after you delete your account.
  • Meal and matching history: 24 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Encrypted backups: replaced or deleted within 35 days.
  • Consent records and access history: 6 years, to prove what was agreed and who viewed health details. They contain no health details.
  • Voice: no audio is kept. Spoken words are turned into text for the command and then discarded.

Security

We use safeguards such as encryption in transit, protected password storage, household-level access controls, access logs and regular reviews. No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your health data, we will notify you and the authorities without unreasonable delay and within the time the law requires (no later than 60 days after discovery).

Children

WAMWins is not directed to children, and accounts are for adults 18 and older. A parent or legal guardian may add a child’s profile and food needs; we ask for the parent’s consent first. Parents can review, change or delete a child’s information, and withdraw consent, at any time. We do not share children’s information with third parties for advertising or any purpose that is not needed to run the service.

HIPAA

WAMWins is a consumer wellness app. DigitalDevWizard is not a health care provider, health plan or health care clearinghouse and does not act for one, so the information you enter is not covered by HIPAA. We still protect your health information with safeguards modeled on the HIPAA Security Rule, and federal and state consumer health privacy laws apply. If we ever offer features for health care providers, those features will operate under a business associate agreement.

Voice commands

Voice commands are an optional feature in some plans. They are off until you turn them on and agree. When your device supports it, speech is recognized on the device. Otherwise your device’s speech service turns the audio into text; that service is provided by your browser or phone maker under their own privacy terms. WAMWins does not keep recordings, does not create voiceprints and does not identify people by their voice.

State privacy rights

Residents of California, Washington, Nevada, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon and other states with consumer privacy laws have the rights described above. California residents: we collect the categories listed in this notice, including sensitive personal information (health), only to provide the service you request; we do not sell or share it. Washington and Nevada residents: see the Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies and ask for your consent again where the law requires.
